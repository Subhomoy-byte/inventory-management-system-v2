# Surface Brief: IWMS Pro Workspace

Main warehouse operator & manager workspace (iwms-ui/src/App.tsx, Dashboard, Inventory, Warehouse Map, Movements, Orders, Cycle Count, Reports).
Mode: operate

## Audience & Job
Warehouse associates and inventory managers carrying out rapid stock movements, monitoring inventory levels, and reconciling floor discrepancies.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An authentic industrial terminal that treats warehouse operations with high-precision engineering clarity, refusing generic crypto-glassmorphic glowing blobs, floating obstructive docks, and artificial monospace costumes.

OWN-WORLD: Tactical slate ground (#0B0F17) with refined structural borders (rgba(255,255,255,0.08)), high-contrast tabular typography, state-rich semantic tokens (signal emerald #10B981 for synced/optimal, amber #F59E0B for low-stock warning, crimson #EF4444 for critical/outbox-error, industrial cyan #06B6D4 for active selection), and tactile micro-interactions (150ms ease-out).
